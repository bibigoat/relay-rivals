THE FLIGHT MANUAL

Win the orbit.
One round at a time.

Take turns placing influence on five relays. At the end of each round, each relay’s unique leader earns its points. The highlighted relay pays 2 extra points. A tied lead earns nothing.

01

Make one move

Deploy, surge, disrupt, charge, or bank. You have 15 seconds; a timeout triggers an automatic move.

02

Watch your energy

Start with 4. Gain 2 each new round, up to 8. Charge adds 3, also capped at 8.

03

Think ahead

Influence decays by 1 after scoring. The starting player and spotlight rotate every round.

04

Finish on top

After 8 rounds, most points wins. Equal scores share the win.

Deploy · 2⚡ Add 1 influence.

Surge · 4⚡ Add 3 influence.

Disrupt · 3⚡ Remove up to 2 enemy influence.

Charge · free Recover 3 energy.

Bank · 3⚡ Gain 2 points immediately.
